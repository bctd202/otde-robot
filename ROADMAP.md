# Roadmap

## Phase 1
Backend/frontend application, mock provider, migrated and seeded database, dashboard, basic liquidity levels, structured setup detection, lottery filtering, persisted demo journal, and basic seeded analytics.

## Phase 2
External alert delivery, economic-event guardrails, correlation/risk controls, and richer grouped analytics.

## Phase 3
A slower day/swing strategy mode with separate timeframes, contracts, holding rules, and validation data. Add a restrictive no-gambling mode with hard risk limits, daily lockouts, and no lottery surface.

## Later research
Full options backtesting when honest historical option quotes exist, advanced paper fills, weekly optimization reports, and richer WebSocket updates.
