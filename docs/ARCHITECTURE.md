# Architecture

The React/Vite client reads cached FastAPI REST responses; a heartbeat WebSocket remains available for a future push transport. A server-owned APScheduler cron job runs at second 5 of each minute, checks the newly completed one-minute candle, scans once, and persists the complete result. The single job instance coalesces missed runs with a short grace period rather than replaying stale work after resume. The browser polls persisted scans and server-owned position marks and cannot multiply provider scans. FastAPI delegates all quotes, candles, chains, and feed metadata through one process-wide caching provider. Quotes are cached by symbol, 1M candles are fetched once per symbol and locally aggregated for slower timeframes, expirations are cached for six hours, and chains are keyed by symbol/expiration. `MockMarketDataProvider` is deterministic and explicitly reports `mode=mock`; the Tradier adapter is read-only, enforces a hard request budget below the upstream limit, categorizes safe upstream failures without logging credentials or response bodies, fails closed, and cannot place orders.

The 1-Min/0DTE engine computes VWAP, opening range, short-term structure, and same-day contracts. The Structured Intraday engine independently requires 1H/VWAP bias, a 15M liquidity sweep, 5M structure shift, controlled retest, 2R target space, and 5–14 DTE contract policy. Both are deterministic and consume the same cached 1M snapshot; no LLM participates in calculations. The signal engine advances strategy-scoped WATCH/BUY/terminal lifecycles, stores tagged alert events, and evaluates open paper/research outcomes. Paper entry reruns the selected strategy and exact contract selection on the server before persisting. SQLAlchemy persists scans, lifecycle state, alerts, market data, setups, paper entities, outcomes, reports, settings, and quality events. Alembic owns schema initialization; SQLite is the local default.

The seed command rebuilds a deterministic demo dataset. React renders provider, engine health, lifecycle validity, account safety, and alert history before secondary market context, and visually isolates the Lottery Lab.
